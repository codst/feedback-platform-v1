Constructive Feedback Framework

Our mission is to help creators receive actionable, kind, and useful peer feedback on their work.

The Three Pillars of Great Feedback

1. What Works Well

Highlight the strengths of the project. What caught your eye? Which interactions, visual choices, or ideas were intuitive or delightful? Starting with positive reinforcement builds confidence and clarity on what should be preserved.

2. What Could Be Improved

Identify areas of friction, confusion, or potential bugs without being harsh. Be specific: instead of "the UI is confusing," say "it wasn't obvious where to click next after filling out the form."

3. Suggestions & Next Steps

Offer concrete ideas or alternatives the creator can experiment with. Suggesting a small tweak, a helpful library, or a fresh perspective often sparks the next breakthrough.
